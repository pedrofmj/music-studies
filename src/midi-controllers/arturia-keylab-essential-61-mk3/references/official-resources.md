## Source Notes

- The official downloads page lists the KeyLab Essential 61 mk3 English manual
  version 1.1.6, firmware version 1.2.1, current DAW integration scripts, and
  the KeyLab Essential mk3 CheatSheet as of the research date.
- The linked official manual is version 1.0.0 and covers both 49- and 61-key
  mk3 models. Its 61-key-relevant hardware, MIDI, and DAW facts support the
  initial capability record.
- Before a firmware update, confirm the version offered for this exact model,
  save the present configuration, and log the observed result in Backups.
