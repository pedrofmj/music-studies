# M-VAVE FM-1

This knowledge base covers the M-VAVE FM-1 pocket FM synthesizer. Unlike the
other M-VAVE folders, the FM-1 generates sound: it combines six FM operators,
effects, a sequencer, and MIDI connectivity in one portable instrument.

## Verified Hardware Summary

- Six-operator FM synthesis with 32 algorithms and 128 factory presets.
- 27-key silicone keyboard, mono mode, and up to 12-voice poly mode.
- Filter, reverb, delay, distortion, chorus, and phaser effect chain.
- Seven-mode arpeggiator and 16-step sequencer with 16 patterns.
- USB-C MIDI, BLE MIDI, 3.5 mm MIDI input, and 3.5 mm stereo audio output.
- Built-in speaker, 2000mAh battery, approximately 12 hours claimed operation.
- 161.5 x 96.5 x 28.6 mm; approximately 251 g.
