## Source Notes

- The official product data describes a six-operator, 32-algorithm FM engine,
  128 factory voices, a 16-step sequencer, and simultaneous USB, BLE, and
  3.5 mm MIDI input.
- The latest FM-1 package listed in the official download data is V14 dated
  2026-07-06. Confirm version, release notes, and recovery procedure before an
  update.
