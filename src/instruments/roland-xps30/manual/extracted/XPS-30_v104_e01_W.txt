XPS-30 Program Update
     This explains how to update the XPS-30’s system program.



Checking the Version                                                                        Update Procedure
     Before you download the system program, check the version of your XPS-30.                Follow the procedure below to install the program “xps30_up.bin” in your XPS-30.

1. Press the [MENU] button.                                                                    NOTE
2. Move the cursor to “SYSTEM,” and press the [ENTER] button.                                   Never turn off the XPS-30’s power while the update is in progress.
3. Move the cursor to “INFORMATION” tab.                                                        Doing so may make the XPS-30’s system unable to start up correctly.
