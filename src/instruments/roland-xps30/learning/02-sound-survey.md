# Sound Survey

Use this learning module to discover which factory and expansion sounds are
useful in the actual worship context. The catalog fields, rating scale, and tag
definitions are in [Sound Catalog Reference](../reference/05-sound-catalog.md).

## Order Of Work

Survey sound families in this order:

1. Acoustic pianos.
2. Pads and strings.
3. Electric pianos.
4. Organs and choirs.
5. Leads, brass, and ambient FX.

Do not catalogue every factory sound before moving on. The first pass should
find usable candidates for the core live setup.

## Per-Sound Exercise

For each candidate:

1. Play alone in low, middle, and high ranges.
2. Test sustain and velocity response.
3. If it is a pad, string, choir, or EP, layer it under a known piano.
4. Listen at rehearsal volume when possible.
5. Record a rating, tags, intended worship use, and the context in which it
   worked or failed.

## Completion Targets

Finish the first sound survey when the records identify:

- Five acoustic piano candidates, including one dependable default.
- Five pads or string sounds, including one low-motion layer.
- Two electric pianos, one organ, and one choir or texture.
- One pad-only fallback and one unlayered piano fallback.

Do not call a sound stage-ready until it has survived a realistic monitoring or
band-volume check.
