# TEYUN A8 Study And Integration

The TEYUN A8 is documented here as an analog mixer with USB audio-interface
capability. Its primary role is to combine and monitor audio from instruments,
microphones, computers, and playback devices; it is not a MIDI controller or a
sound generator.

## Source Status

No manufacturer documentation was located during the initial research on
2026-07-27. The current facts come from third-party Manuals+ product/manual
pages, which contain conflicting statements about USB recording and channel
layout. They are retained as **provisional** facts only. See
[Manual Inventory](manual/manuals.md) and [Source Assessment](references/sources.md).

Before a live setup, compare this documentation against the printed manual and
the labels on the actual unit. Add photos and observed tests to `images/` and
`routing/`.
