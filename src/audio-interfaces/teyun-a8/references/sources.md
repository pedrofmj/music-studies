## Rules Until Confirmed

- Treat all port and USB descriptions as `provisional_third_party`.
- Do not enable +48 V around keyboard, FM-1, phone, or computer connections.
- Do not promise multitrack, loopback, effects-in-USB, or a particular USB
  channel count without an observed Ubuntu test.
- Capture the printed manual or front/rear-panel photos before changing the
  source status to `documented`.
